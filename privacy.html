PRIVACY

Accounts are optional. Privacy is essential.

Visitors can still use SkillUP without a cloud account. Signed-in features collect only the information needed to connect learning, assignments and progress.

Guest use on this device

An optional guest learner profile stores the nickname, school year, activity completion, writing drafts and recent scores in the browser’s local storage. Clearing this website’s browser data removes that local information.

Information used for an account

When account services are enabled, SkillUP stores the verified email address, display name, role and—where relevant—school year. It also stores class membership, assignments, due dates, submissions, scores, teacher feedback and approved parent–student connections. SkillUP does not ask for a date of birth, home address or precise location.

Who can see learning information

  • Students can see their own classes, assignments, submissions and feedback.
  • Approved teachers can see only classes they created and students enrolled in those classes.
  • Parents and carers can see read-only information only for children linked by a private, expiring family code.
  • Teacher accounts remain pending until they are reviewed.

Database access rules enforce these boundaries; account roles are not trusted from browser storage.

Children’s privacy

A parent, carer or school should supervise account creation for younger learners. Use a suitable display name and do not enter a school name, address, contact details or sensitive personal information in class names, instructions, writing drafts or feedback.

Service providers

The public website is hosted by GitHub Pages and uses Google Fonts. When accounts are enabled, Supabase provides authentication and database services. These providers may receive standard technical request information such as IP address and browser details under their own policies. SkillUP does not include advertising or behavioural advertising trackers.

Security and account choices

Email verification, password reset links and database access controls protect account information. No internet service can promise absolute security. A private contact method and account-deletion process must be published before account registration is enabled for the public.
