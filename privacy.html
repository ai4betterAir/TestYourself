PRIVACY

No account is required.

SkillUP is designed to minimise the personal information needed for learning activities.

Information stored on your device

If you create an optional learner profile, the nickname, selected school year, activity completion and recent scores are stored in your browser’s local storage. Writing drafts may also be stored there when the learner selects Save. SkillUP does not set an automatic retention period because this information remains under the user’s browser controls.

What SkillUP does not request

  • No email address, password, date of birth or school name is required.
  • No cloud learner account is created by this website.
  • No advertising, behavioural analytics or location-tracking scripts are included.
  • The support form creates text for the user to copy; it does not transmit the text to SkillUP.

Children’s privacy

Children should not enter their full name, school, address, contact information or other identifying details into a nickname, writing draft or issue report. A parent or carer should supervise younger learners and submit public reports on their behalf.

External services

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages and uses Google Fonts. When a page loads, those providers may receive standard technical request information such as an IP address and browser details under their own policies. Selecting the GitHub report link opens GitHub, where GitHub’s own account and privacy terms apply.

Removing saved progress

Users can remove locally saved SkillUP information by clearing this website’s browser data. Clearing browser data may remove all saved progress and drafts and cannot be undone by SkillUP.
