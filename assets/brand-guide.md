Use rounded cards, soft blue backgrounds, generous white space, Fredoka for display headings and Nunito for body text. Keep the interface cheerful but uncluttered. The compact book/flame mark is used for favicons and small headers; the full logo lock-up is used on registration, key landing sections and footer branding.
