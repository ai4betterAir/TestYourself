Subject accents: Maths blue, Vocabulary purple, English orange, Selective violet.
Use rounded cards, tinted subject washes, generous white space, Fredoka for display headings and Nunito for body text. Keep the interface cheerful and uncluttered. Primary action colour is warm orange.
