FOR PARENTS AND CARERS

Short, supported practice.

SkillUP works best when a learner chooses the correct school year, reads the Learn section first and uses feedback to understand mistakes.

Suggested use

  • Begin with one topic and keep practice sessions manageable.
  • Encourage the learner to use hints during Practice.
  • Use Test mode only after the learner understands the examples.
  • Review incorrect answers together and return to Learn when needed.
  • Move to a lower or higher year only when the current level is clearly unsuitable.

Scores

A SkillUP score shows performance on one generated practice set. It is not a school grade, diagnosis or official Selective Test result.

Supervision and wellbeing

Young learners should use the website with appropriate adult guidance. Encourage regular breaks and stop an activity if the learner becomes tired or frustrated.
