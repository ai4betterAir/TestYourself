TERMS OF USE

Use SkillUP responsibly.

By using SkillUP, you agree to use it as a supplementary educational resource and in a safe, lawful way.

Educational use

SkillUP provides general learning and practice activities. It does not replace professional teaching, formal school assessment or educational advice tailored to an individual learner. An adult should review whether a year level and activity are suitable for a particular child.

Accounts and roles

Provide accurate account information, protect the password and use only the role that applies to you. Students must not use another learner’s account. A person registering as a teacher must be authorised to work with the relevant learners; teacher tools may remain unavailable until the account is reviewed. Parents and carers may access only children they are authorised to support.

Assignments and results

Teachers are responsible for choosing suitable activities, instructions and due dates. SkillUP scores reflect only the questions submitted through the website and are not a school grade, diagnosis or official examination result. Reasonable care is taken when preparing questions and explanations, but errors may still occur.

Original content and permitted use

Unless stated otherwise, SkillUP text, questions, explanations, branding and website code are provided for personal and classroom learning. Do not republish substantial parts, remove attribution, sell the material or present SkillUP content as official examination material without permission.

Acceptable use

Do not interfere with the website, attempt to access another person’s account or data, introduce harmful code, misuse reports, automatically extract substantial content, impersonate a teacher or submit private information without authority.

Availability and external services

SkillUP may change, pause or remove features and cannot promise uninterrupted availability. Links to external websites are provided for convenience. Those services have their own terms and privacy practices.

Changes

Content, features and these terms may be updated as SkillUP improves. Continued use after an update means the current terms apply.
