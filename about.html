ABOUT SKILLUP

Learn with confidence.

SkillUP is an independent learning website for primary students. It provides clear teaching, focused practice and tests across Maths, English, Vocabulary and Selective preparation.

How learning works

Activities are designed around a simple path: learn the idea, practise the skill, complete a test and use feedback to improve. Content is grouped by Australian school year.

Educational scope

SkillUP is a supplementary practice resource. It does not replace classroom teaching, professional tutoring or a school’s formal assessment. Parents and teachers should use their judgement when selecting a level.

Independent resource

SkillUP is not affiliated with the NSW Department of Education, NESA or the official NSW Selective High School Placement Test. Its practice questions and learning explanations are created for SkillUP and are not official test questions.
